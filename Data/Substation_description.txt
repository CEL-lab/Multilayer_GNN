
This file contains geospatial and attribute information for 709 electrical substations.  
Each row represents a substation site within the utility’s service territory.  
The columns are described below:

1. **X** – Longitude of the substation location (decimal degrees, west longitude is negative).

2. **Y** – Latitude of the substation location (decimal degrees, positive values indicate north latitude).

3. **Z** – Elevation value (zero for all records; elevation is not provided).

4. **Name** – Name of the substation, typically including an identifier and station number (e.g., “Seminole Pump 7319”).

5. **ADDRESSLIN** – Primary address line for the substation (street address or driving directions).  

6. **ADDRESSL_1** – Additional address information, such as notes on feeders or ownership.

7. **ADDRESSL_2** – Secondary address line; used for construction or metering notes.

8. **ASBUILTSTA** – As‑built status indicator; often “A” for active or as‑built.

9. **DRIVINGDIR** – Narrative driving directions to the substation.

10. **FID** – Unique feature identifier for the GIS record.

11. **Field_1** – Duplicate of the as‑built status used by the GIS software.

12. **INSTALLATI** – Installation date of the substation (string format such as “1/1/99”).

13. **LABELTEXT** – Text label used for map displays; usually the station name without the number.

14. **MAINTAINED** – Organization responsible for maintaining the substation (e.g., the utility itself, Arkansas Valley Electric Cooperative (AVEC), Western Farmers Electric Cooperative (WFEC), etc.).

15. **OWNER** – Ownership indicator (0 or 1), where 1 may represent ownership by the reporting utility and 0 indicates another owner.

16. **PLANTCLASS** – Classification of the substation: ‘D’ for distribution, ‘T’ for transmission, ‘DD’ for dual‑purpose distribution, ‘P’ for pumping or plant, etc.

17. **PLANTSTATU** – Operational status of the substation: ‘A’ for active, ‘S’ for spare, ‘H’ for historical or retired.

18. **PMU** – Indicator (0 or 1) showing whether a phasor measurement unit is installed at the substation.

19. **POINT_X** – Duplicate longitude coordinate used by the GIS system.

20. **POINT_Y** – Duplicate latitude coordinate used by the GIS system.

21. **STATIONNAM** – Numeric station name identifier (station number).

22. **STATIONNUM** – Duplicate numeric station number (same as **STATIONNAM**).

23. **SUBTYPECD** – Subtype code for the facility (0 or 1) used by the GIS system.

24. **SYMBOLROTA** – Rotation angle (degrees) for the station’s symbol on maps.

25. **TOWN** – Town or locality where the substation is located; blank for rural stations.

26. **Voltage** – Nominal operating voltage of the substation in kilovolts (kV).  Values range from 0 kV to 500 kV.

27. **WORKFLOWST** – Workflow status code; 0 indicates normal status.

28. **WORKFUNCTI** – Workflow function code; 0 indicates a base function for the GIS record.
